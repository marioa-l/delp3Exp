==========================================================================
TIME-TO-EXACT GROUND TRUTH - FINAL RESULTS
==========================================================================

Ground truth: for each query, both samplers run until they reach the
exact interval or hit a safety cap. The winner is whichever reaches
the exact interval first.

-- Dataset --
  Total queries              : 2357
  Resolved (used below)      : 1890
  Unresolved (both hit cap)  : 467
  Configurations             : 15
  Distinct models            : 997

  Caps applied: 900 s first pass; the 575 queries still unresolved
  were re-run at 3600 s, which resolved 108 of them (18.8%).

  Class balance: worlds 1687 (89.3%) | programs 203 (10.7%)

-- Model comparison --
  model                                        accuracy  rec:prog  rec:worlds
  Majority baseline (always worlds)  0.8926 +/- 0.0071     0.000       1.000
  Abstract tree (5-min budget label) 0.7836 +/- 0.0095     0.005       0.877
  Comparison tree (hand-written)     0.9079 +/- 0.0067     0.645       0.940
  Depth-1 tree (time-to-exact label) 0.9524 +/- 0.0049     0.842       0.966

  Accuracy standard errors are binomial. The depth-1 tree's number is
  out-of-fold under GroupKFold(5) grouped by (config, model), so no
  literal of a model appears in both train and test; across folds it
  is 0.9524 +/- 0.0143 (std over folds).

  Accuracy alone is misleading here: the majority baseline already
  scores 0.893. Recall on the minority class is the informative number.

-- The learned rule --
  space_log_ratio = n_annots - em_var       (log2 of the ratio between
                                             the program and world
                                             sampling space sizes)

      space_log_ratio <= -8  ->  sample programs
      space_log_ratio >  -8  ->  sample worlds

    ratio <= -8 (-> programs)    n=  229  purity=0.7467 +/- 0.0287
    ratio >  -8 (-> worlds)      n= 1661  purity=0.9807 +/- 0.0034

  The -8 cut is not fitted on this data: the independent 5-minute
  training set selects -8 on its own, and it transfers here unchanged.
